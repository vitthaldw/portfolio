Vitthal, on location — Punjab

I started out photographing my friends' bands from the side of the stage, trying to get one frame that felt like the room sounded. That's still the job — chasing the version of a moment that has weight to it.

Today that means moving between weddings, concerts, portrait sittings and fashion sets, with a camera in one hand and, more often than not, a gimbal in the other. Every project gets the same question first: what does this person or place actually feel like, before I decide how to shoot it.
